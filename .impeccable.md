## Design Context

### Users
Broader public users who want to use an iPhone as a reliable remote keyboard for a Mac over Wi-Fi. They need low-friction setup, confidence during pairing, and clear feedback while typing.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, premium.
The product should feel trustworthy, controlled, and polished rather than playful or experimental.

### Aesthetic Direction
iOS-only interface with a spacious, refined control layout. Visual hierarchy should prioritize connection state and typing surface with clean grouping, generous whitespace, and subtle material depth. No noisy decoration or dense utility-panel look.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity of current state over decorative elements.
2. Use generous spacing between groups and tighter spacing within groups.
3. Maintain a calm, premium rhythm with consistent structure and restrained accents.
4. Keep interactions obvious and low-friction for first-time users.
5. Preserve functional speed while improving perceived polish.
